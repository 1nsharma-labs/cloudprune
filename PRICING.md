# CloudPrune Pricing & Editions

CloudPrune follows an **Open-Core** model. The CLI scanner, visual HTML report generator, and dry-run cleanup are **100% Free and Open-Source under Apache 2.0**.

For teams that want continuous scheduled automation, multi-account scanning across AWS Organizations / GCP Folders, and Slack alerts, we offer commercial **Enterprise** licenses.
